Your picks from page C, all in the toon style

On page C you picked Glim the Ghost, Darth Jar-Jar, Batman and Possessed Chef Enzo and asked for them redrawn in the toon style for all four variants. Each row is one figure; each column is one of the four directions (A to D), now all built on the toon render (cel shading, Blender 4.5). Every cell shows the figure large, then at 192 px (desktop and Chrome app icon, shown at 96 css px) and 48 px (favicon), on light and on dark.

As agreed on 28 Nov, the three franchise picks are not copied. Each slot has an original figure of the same type, with its own outline, colours and emblem, pushed so it reads at a glance even at 48 px: Captain Clutch (a brooding caped guardian of the night) for Batman, Lord Grumble (a clumsy, comic dark lord) for Darth Jar-Jar, and Chef Gusto (a chef taken over by a ghost) for Possessed Chef Enzo. Glim stays as he was.

About the figures

All four figures are original Brick Tracker characters. The picks they replace are franchise characters, named on each row with their owners: Batman (DC), Darth Jar-Jar (Lucasfilm) and Possessed Chef Enzo (LEGO Hidden Side).

An original figure such as Glim is the safe pick for a published app. The minifigure shape itself is a LEGO trademark, so check that before publishing any of them.

Pick a figure (question 1) and a variant (question 2) below: the row and the column light up, and the cell where they meet is your logo.
