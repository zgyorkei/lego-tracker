Brick Tracker logo proposals20 minifigure busts (14 originals, 6 franchise figures), four directions

A: Glossy studio plasticB: Toon with outlinesC: Round badgeD: App icon tile

Direction C: Round badge

The toon figure in a round emblem with BRICK TRACKER around the top. At 48 px the text would be a blur, so the favicon size drops it and lets the figure fill the badge.

Every figure is a real minifigure bust (head, shoulders and the top of the torso) built from LDraw parts in Blender 4.5, with the face and torso prints painted by the script. Each card shows it large, then at 192 px (desktop and Chrome app icon) and 48 px (favicon), on light and on dark.

About the figures

The first 14 figures are original characters made for Brick Tracker. The last 6 are the franchise characters you asked for (Darth Vader, Darth Jar-Jar, Batman, Jack Davids, Parker L. Jackson and Possessed Chef Enzo), each tagged with its owner below: they carry LEGO, Lucasfilm and DC marks (Star Wars and its characters are Lucasfilm's, Batman is DC's, Hidden Side and its cast are the LEGO Group's). They are fine for your own copy of the app, not for a published one.

The safest picks for a published app are the three Brick Tracker mascots: Bricksy the Builder, Tally the Cataloguer, Studbot. The minifigure shape itself is a LEGO trademark, so check that before publishing any of them.

1. Which figures do you pick?

Tick "Pick this one" on as many figures as you like.
